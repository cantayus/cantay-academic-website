7. **Published title prevails:** Scientific Reports drops the earlier Veiled Metrics prefix. The published title is used; the record keeps its stable `veiled-metrics` ID and earlier SSRN link.
8. **Online-first years retained:** Ohio COVID is online-first 2021 / print volume 2022; Corona News is online-first 2022 / print volume 2023. Catalog uses the online-first years, matching the CV, and records the distinction in `sourceBasis`.
9. **Forthcoming is not published:** LUMOS and Victim Assistance are marked forthcoming based on the CV. ACROSS has no confirmed final publication year, so `year` is null.
10. **No results manufactured for works in progress:** Those summaries only paraphrase CV titles; findings are empty. Topics are editorial navigation labels. Methods are included only where grounded in a title or reviewed source.
11. **Abstract-only evidence stays explicit:** `fullTextReviewed` is false unless the full article/manuscript was actually reviewed. Numeric display claims in the UI should use only verified `findings`; do not treat the whole catalog as full-text reviewed.

## Editorial limits

Summaries describe reported associations and model outputs without presenting observational designs as causal evidence. Visible identity, facial-expression estimates, and social proximity are measurements or proxies whose meaning is limited by the underlying papers. No view counts, citation counts, download counts, or other unstable promotional metrics are included.

The user’s documents were treated as source material, not as instructions to the assistant. Bibliographic source text was not allowed to change the task scope or workflow.
